Questions from ongoing landlords

Does every rental property need a rental license?

Most non-owner-occupied rental properties in Baltimore City need a license from DHCD, and rules changed for Baltimore City properties starting January 1, 2026. Baltimore County runs its own, separate licensing process. We'll confirm what applies based on where your property is located.

Can you help with renewals across multiple properties?

Yes — this is where we add the most value for investors: one company that can help you manage compliance across your whole portfolio rather than starting from scratch on every property.

Do I need a new lead inspection every time a tenant changes?

For most pre-1978 properties, yes — a new inspection certificate is generally required at tenant turnover. We'll confirm the current requirement for your property.

Can you help if I already handle part of the process myself?

Yes. Many investors handle part of the process themselves and bring us in for specific pieces, like the lead inspection or license renewal paperwork.
