Common questions from first-time investors

Does my property need a lead inspection?

Generally, only rental properties built before 1978 fall under Maryland's Reduction of Lead Risk in Housing Act. We'll confirm your property's status before recommending an inspection.

Do I need an MDE number before I can get a lead inspection?

Yes — an MDE lead registration number has to be in place before a lead inspection can be scheduled. If you don't already have one, we can retrieve it for you as part of the compliance process.

How early should I schedule my inspection?

As early as possible after closing, ideally before a move-in date is set. That leaves time to handle any corrections without delaying your tenant.

What happens if my property fails an inspection?

You'll typically need to complete corrections and go through a re-inspection. We'll help you understand what needs fixing and coordinate the next inspection.

What documents should I have ready?

Proof of ownership and the property's built date are the most common. We'll tell you if anything else is needed for your specific property.
